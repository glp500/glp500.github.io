How can a group know more than any of its members? This research field follows the local interactions that produce global order—from animal swarms and markets to scientific communities and online publics.

The aim is not only to identify emergent patterns, but to ask what those patterns make possible, whose objectives they serve, and how their apparent intelligence should be evaluated.
