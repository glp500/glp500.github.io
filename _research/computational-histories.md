Technical systems arrive with histories inside them. Tracing those histories—of classification, calculation, labor, and exchange—helps reveal which assumptions have hardened into infrastructure.

This research combines computational methods with historical interpretation to make archival material more accessible without stripping away the context that gives it meaning.
