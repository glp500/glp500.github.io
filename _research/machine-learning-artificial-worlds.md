Machine learning is both a research method and an object of study. I am interested in models that learn through interaction, the social worlds represented by datasets, and tools that remain legible to the communities using them.

This field includes practical work in language models, multimodal representations, evaluation, and the design of computational systems for complex real-world contexts.
