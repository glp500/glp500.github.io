Cybernetics offers a language for systems that sense, compare, and act. I use that language critically: to understand not only how feedback enables learning, but how measurements and goals reshape the worlds they claim to describe.

This work connects historical cybernetic ideas to contemporary computational systems, emphasizing legibility, adaptation, and the unintended effects of designed feedback.
