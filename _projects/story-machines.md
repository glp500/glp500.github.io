Story Machines is a project and early-stage venture building local AI tools for institutional digital ecosystems. It focuses on helping organizations connect, interpret, and work with their own knowledge while preserving provenance, privacy, and the social context that gives information meaning.

The work combines technical prototyping with a larger design question: what should an intelligent institutional memory feel like to the people who sustain it?
