Write the post in Markdown. Valid `kind` values are Announcement, Essay,
Project Update, and Reading Note; `kind` drives the homepage filters.

Every post appears on the homepage newest-first. Reading time, share links,
and previous/next navigation are generated automatically. Any field left
blank or empty is skipped entirely, so a plain text post needs nothing
beyond `title`, `date`, `kind`, and `excerpt`.
